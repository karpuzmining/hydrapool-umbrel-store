      Coinbase

      100 basis points is 1%. Leave the fee at 0 to pay miners the whole block. Your address is the bootstrap payout for the first seconds, before any shares exist.

      Install Umbrel's Tailscale app, open it, and paste this machine's tailnet name. Friends use that name with the stratum port on the Connect tab (34533). Leave it blank until Tailscale is connected.

      Difficulty

      1024 is a sane start for small ASIC miners. The pool can still lower a worker toward the minimum. Bitaxe-class miners are fine at these defaults.

      Saving rewrites the pool config and restarts Hydrapool. Miners reconnect on their own. You can switch modes later from this tab.
